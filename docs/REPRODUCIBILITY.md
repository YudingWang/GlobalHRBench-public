# Evaluation and reproduction

Run `python scripts/verify_release.py` from the repository root after installing `requirements.txt`. The command reads released files only, performs no network requests, and does not edit responses or scores.

## What the check verifies

1. Every file in `MANIFEST.json` matches its SHA-256 hash; there are 501 canonical questions, 138 reviewed questions, and 1,104 human-scored outputs.
2. Required-rule metrics are recomputed from final atom annotations using the original alternative-path logic. Correctness and fidelity scores are derived from the released human labels, with the original failure policy.
3. Human question-weighted, equal-fact-group, equal-dependency-group, language, and evidence-available aggregates match their saved tables. Missing/unclear sensitivity tables and the paired 2,000-replicate intervals (seed 20261105) are checked.
4. Pre-adjudication exact agreement and Cohen's kappa are recomputed from 2,208 anonymous initial forms. The disagreement/unclear rule identifies 113 cases requiring adjudication. This validates arithmetic, not the reviewers' professional judgment or independence.
5. Raw final-label joint counts are recomputed separately from protocol-adjusted rates.
6. The original automated scorers re-score 900 canonical predictions from the first five evidence-access models plus 600 predictions from the two added models. All 840 retrieval pairs and 1,260 evidence-access item scores are reaggregated. Broad paired source-group intervals use 5,000 replicates and seed 31, preserving the original method.

No new model response, annotation, or experimental outcome is generated by these checks. Statistical functions in `evaluation/human_metrics.py`, `broad_metrics.py`, and `agreement.py` are extracted verbatim from the frozen research implementation; the automatic scorers and schemas are copied unchanged. `PROVENANCE.json` records their hashes and extraction scope.

## Metrics and conditions

- **Automated target-answer score:** study-specific structured checks and lexical matching. It is not a full-answer human correctness judgment.
- **Human correctness:** whether the response answers the requested legal issue, including applicable scope and qualification.
- **Full-rule completeness:** whether a valid required-rule path is completely covered. Average atom coverage alone is insufficient.
- **Source fidelity:** whether supplied evidence supports all material legal claims, including additional claims. Unsupported does not mean legally false.
- **C / `public_cue_bm25_top5`:** retrieved evidence, using visible jurisdiction cues.
- **D / `gold_designated_evidence`:** designated evidence and required dependencies.

The three broad conditions are `closed`, `rag` (BM25), and `oracle` (the historical internal label for designated evidence). Their scores and denominators are kept separate from human validation. Evidence-available subsets are descriptive; they are not randomized evidence interventions.

## What is not covered

The twelve original retrieval models do not have a complete retained set of raw answers, so that cohort supports reaggregation of saved item scores rather than complete answer rescoring. The main human answers, 2,208 anonymous initial forms, and 1,104 released final labels are included; original spreadsheets, signature metadata, and adjudicator identities are private. This release does not reconstruct the adjudicator's decision process from private workbooks.

The quick check does not rebuild the original five-model 423-question resource-wide analysis or the separate six-model response-contract diagnostic in the appendix. Their reported tables remain in the unchanged paper source. Do not interpret a successful check as independent verification of every ancillary experiment, legal proposition, or consent record.

## Build the paper

```bash
cd paper/source
tectonic --keep-logs --untrusted GlobalHRBench_preprint.tex
```

The source includes ACL styles, bibliography, BBL, value macros, tables, and vector figures. It was independently extracted and compiled with Tectonic. An offline rebuild requires Tectonic's TeX dependencies to be cached. arXiv-server compilation has not been tested. The paper uses a nonanonymous two-column preprint layout; no conference acceptance is claimed.
