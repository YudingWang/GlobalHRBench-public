# Rights and privacy

The repository's MIT license applies to software authored for this project. It does not expand rights in third-party source texts, translations, or provider-generated content. Source records retain attribution URLs and their recorded provenance. No blanket CC0 or Creative Commons license is asserted for all dataset content; users should check the relevant source terms before redistribution.

This public snapshot includes research questions, source-linked annotations, model response text, and anonymous review judgments. It excludes reviewer names, original Office files and their author metadata, private consultation and payment records, provider-account details, API credentials, and private Git history. Reviewer slots do not identify individuals.

The author affiliations are supplied by the authors. They do not constitute institutional endorsement of the benchmark or its results.

Questions, corrections, and reports of material that should not be distributed can be raised through GitHub issues without posting additional personal or confidential information. This benchmark supports research, not individual legal or employment decisions.
